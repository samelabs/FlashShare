We offer two ways to sign in: a local account (username and password) or X OAuth. A local account stores your username, email address, and password hash, along with account and profile fields you provide or that are required to run the service. When you sign in with X, we store the X account identifiers and public profile fields used for account binding and display; we do not read your timeline, direct messages, or following list, and we never post on your behalf.

Upload and tool-processing records store the file name, size, download link, expiry time, upload time, and basic access origin, used for quota control, metered settlement, auditing, and troubleshooting.

Earnings from tools you publish and your consumption records are kept as ledger entries, used for settlement, withdrawals, and reconciliation. After content expires or is deleted, some records may be retained for settlement and auditing purposes.
