1. The Service

518.PLUS is an MCP-native tools platform. Tools are published once and can be used through two equivalent surfaces: the Web interface, which is the surface for human use, and the MCP (Model Context Protocol) endpoint, which is the machine surface for agents and automation. Both surfaces run the same tool invocation pipeline and the same accounting.

2. Accounts

You can use the service with a local account (username and password) or by signing in with X (OAuth). You are responsible for the activity carried out under your account, for keeping your credentials confidential, and for the accuracy of the information you provide.

3. API Tokens

The platform lets you create API tokens (prefixed fsk_) for authenticating MCP calls. A token is shown to you in full only once, at creation. You are responsible for storing your tokens securely and for all usage carried out with them. If a token is compromised, revoke it from your account page. Do not share tokens publicly or embed them in shared code.

4. Files, Processing and Temporary Sharing

You can upload files, process them with tools, and share the results as temporary links. Each share is created with an expiry period and a download limit that you choose; when the expiry passes or the download limit is reached, the link stops working and the file is removed by routine cleanup. You are responsible for ensuring that the content you upload and process is lawful, that you hold the rights needed to use it, and that it contains no malicious code.

5. Tools: First-Party and Third-Party

Tools are provided by the platform (first-party tools) or by other developers (third-party tools). A third-party tool runs at an HTTP endpoint controlled by its developer. By using a third-party tool you acknowledge that its processing logic, availability and output are defined and controlled by its developer, not by the platform. The price of a paid tool is shown before use.

6. What a Third-Party HTTP Tool May Receive

To fulfil your request, a third-party HTTP tool may receive what is necessary for the job: a time-limited, signed download URL for each source file, together with the file name, content type and size, and the tool parameters you supplied. The tool's developer downloads the files from these URLs and returns the output. Do not use third-party tools with files you are not willing to expose to that developer.

7. Credits, Membership and Paid Tools

Use of metered tools is paid with credits. Credits are purchased or obtained through membership plans; membership may also change usage allowances such as upload quotas, expiry periods and download limits. Paid tool use is metered per call at the price shown before use. Failed calls are settled according to the platform's accounting rules; the ledger is the record of these movements.

Withdrawing earnings is a settlement capability operated by the platform: withdrawal requests enter a review queue and are processed by the platform. The platform deducts its commission from tool revenue according to the published rates.
